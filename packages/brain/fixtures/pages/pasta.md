Pasta is a food made from durum wheat and water. It is served with tomato sauce, cheese,
or olive oil. Cooking pasta requires boiling water and salt.

## Cooking tips

Use a large pot, salt the water, and drain the pasta when it is al dente.

## Pairings

Pasta pairs well with red wine, garlic bread, and fresh basil.
